## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Planning lives on Plan tab
Nutrition quick-log on Log SHALL remain the capture surface for meals, photo estimate, and hydration quick-add. Weekly meal planning, draft generate, and grocery SHALL live on Plan → Nutrition rather than expanding Log into a planning dashboard.

#### Scenario: Log does not become weekly planner
- **WHEN** the athlete opens Log with tracking enabled
- **THEN** they can quick-log intake and are not required to manage the weekly meal plan grid on Log
